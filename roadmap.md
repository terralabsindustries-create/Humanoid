Product Roadmap

Roadmap Principles

Validate the complete customer journey before expanding breadth.

Prioritize reliable workflow execution over feature count.

Start with one primary industry while keeping the architecture configurable.

Build strong human handoff, auditability, and testing from the beginning.

Avoid premature enterprise complexity.

MVP — Reliable AI Receptionist

Goal

Enable a small healthcare or appointment-based business to create and deploy an AI phone receptionist that answers calls, handles common questions, books appointments, and escalates safely.

Primary Industry

Start with a dental clinic or outpatient clinic because the workflow is understandable, appointment-driven, and valuable while still exposing real operational complexity.
